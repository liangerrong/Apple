## Note on GAAP vs Adjusted EPS
- FY2024 GAAP EPS includes one-time EU tax charge reducing net income; FY2025 benefits from lower tax rate (15.6% vs 24.1%)
- When comparing YoY EPS growth, always use adjusted basis for Q4 YoY comparisons
- All figures in this research use GAAP unless explicitly noted

---

## Note on Services Revenue Definition
- Apple reports Services net of content costs (i.e., App Store revenue is reported net of developer payouts on Apple's commission take)
- Apple does NOT disclose gross App Store GMV as revenue — GMV was $406B US alone but Apple's reported Services revenue reflects only Apple's cut
- This is consistent with ASC 606 / GAAP standards; no misrepresentation
