## 1.1 Corporate Overview

Apple Inc. (NASDAQ: AAPL) is the world's largest technology company by market capitalization (~$3.9T as of late 2025), headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and a growing portfolio of digital services. Its vertically integrated model — from silicon design (Apple Silicon, M-series, A-series chips) to software (iOS, macOS, watchOS) to retail — is a defining strategic feature.

**Fiscal year**: October 1 – September 30 (FY2025 ended September 27, 2025).

## 1.6 Organizational Structure

Apple operates as a single reporting entity with no separate "segment" profitability disclosure (only revenue by product line and gross margin by Products vs. Services). Key operating groups:
- **Hardware**: iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables/Home/Accessories
- **Services**: App Store, Apple Music, Apple TV+, Apple Arcade, iCloud, Apple Pay, Apple Card, AppleCare, advertising, licensing
- **Technology**: Apple Silicon design, software platforms (iOS/macOS/iPadOS/watchOS/visionOS)
- **Retail**: ~500+ Apple Stores globally + apple.com

## Impact Analysis

**So What**: Apple is structurally transforming from a hardware company to a hardware-enabled services platform. Services reached 26.2% of revenue in FY2025 (up from ~22% in FY2023) and contributes disproportionately to profitability at 75.4% gross margin.

**Now What**: The trajectory of Services share expansion is the single most important metric to track. If Services continues at 14% CAGR vs. total company 6-7% growth, Services will exceed 30% of revenue by FY2027 — materially improving blended margins.

**What If**: If iPhone revenue growth slows below 3% (China pressure + AI competition) while Services growth holds, Apple's mix shift accelerates — actually positive for margins but may compress absolute revenue growth optically.

**Compared To**: Apple's revenue base ($416B) is smaller than Amazon ($717B) but its profitability ($112B net income) vastly exceeds Amazon ($59B). Apple's business model is more comparable to Microsoft's model of hardware-enabled platform monetization.
