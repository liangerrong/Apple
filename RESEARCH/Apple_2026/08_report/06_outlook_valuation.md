## Impact Analysis

**So What**: At ~$260/share, Apple trades near fair value on a base case ($249 target) and at a meaningful discount to bull case ($310). The market is pricing roughly a 50/50 mix of base and bull — which implicitly assumes no catastrophic Google deal termination or significant EU DMA acceleration.

**Now What**: The key near-term catalyst is Q2 FY2026 earnings (May 7, 2026). If gross margin prints at 48%+ (within guidance range) and Services grows 14%+, the base case trajectory holds. A miss below 47% gross margin (tariff headwinds) would pressure the stock.

**What If**: If the DOJ prohibits Apple-Google search revenue sharing (ruling expected in 2026), our base case EPS drops ~$1.50 to ~$6.25 — implying a P/E re-rating to ~$150-180 range, assuming the market prices this as a permanent impairment. This is the single biggest binary risk in the model.

**Compared To**: Apple at 33x P/E (FY2026) trades at a premium to Alphabet (20x) but discount to Microsoft (34x). The premium vs. Alphabet reflects Apple's superior business model quality (less advertising dependency, stronger moat) but the discount vs. Microsoft reflects Services' regulatory risk and Apple's lower operating margin.
