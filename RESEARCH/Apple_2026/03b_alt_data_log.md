# 03b Alternative Alpha Data Log

## Purpose
Capture grassroots / alternative data signals that lead or diverge from reported financials.

---

## Findings

### Real Demand — iPhone Resale Prices & Secondary Market
**Source**: Swappa (via LinkedIn post, April 2025), TechBuyers (2025), Swappa current prices (March 2026)
**Signal**: POSITIVE — Apple dominates secondary market
- Apple iPhones dominated Swappa's best-seller list in April 2025 (iPhone 13 #1, iPhone 14 #2, iPhone 12 #3)
- iPhone 11 (2019) still selling briskly at ~$190 "good condition" — brand durability
- iPhones retain 45–60% of original value after 2 years — 2–3x Android retention rate
- iPhone 15 Pro Max 256GB trading at ~$710 (Q2 2025)
- Secondary market demand for iPhones booming; expected 45M+ used iPhones sold in 2025
**Divergence from reported figures?**: NO — confirms strong brand moat and premium positioning; consistent with ASP uplift and upgrade cycle

### Pricing Power — iPhone ASP Trend
**Source**: SAG via Morningstar (Jan 2026), CIRP US data (Feb 2026), Counterpoint (Jan 2026)
**Signal**: STRONGLY POSITIVE — iPhone ASP broke $1,000 for first time
- Global iPhone ASP hit $1,032 in Q4 2025 (+11.5% YoY)
- US weighted average retail price Dec 2025: $1,077 (highest ever)
- iPhone 17 Pro base price increased to $1,099; iPhone 17 Air $100 more than Plus
- 56% of iPhone 17 buyers chose >base storage (UBS data); incremental storage at ~75% gross margin
- Pro model mix: ~40% of installed base now Pro or Pro Max
**Divergence?**: NO — confirms H2 (hardware margins sustainable). Pricing power intact.

### Org Expansion — Apple AI/ML Hiring
**Source**: LinkedIn posts, Apple Careers page, CNBC reporting (2026)
**Signal**: POSITIVE — major AI/ML investment ramp
- Apple committed $600B US investment over 4 years (including AI infrastructure)
- Planned to hire 20,000 for R&D, silicon engineering, software development, AI and ML
- AI servers shipping from Houston manufacturing facility (originally planned 2026, started early)
- Apple Careers page has active ML Engineer, Apple Intelligence roles listed
- R&D spend +10% YoY to $34.6B (FY2025) — signals genuine investment, not narrative
**Divergence?**: MINOR TENSION — R&D investment is real; but Siri AI output has been delayed. Watch gap between hiring signal and product delivery.

### Product Quality — Apple Intelligence Feature Reception
**Source**: CNBC (Dec 2025), Okoone/ArtJoker analysis, TIKR (Feb 2026)
**Signal**: MIXED — features deployed but flagship AI Siri delayed
- Basic Apple Intelligence features (Writing Tools, Image Playground, Genmoji, priority notifications) launched FY2025
- Contextual "on-screen" Siri and multi-step command features delayed to 2026
- Tim Cook Q4 FY2025: "majority of Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone users actively engaging with AI features" — but this covers lightweight features
- CNBC Dec 2025: iPhone 16 AI supercycle "did not materialize"; Apple admits Siri is a year behind ChatGPT/Gemini
- iPhone 17 upgrade driven by hardware design (Air form factor, camera) not AI
**Divergence?**: YES — MAJOR SIGNAL. "AI supercycle" narrative vs actual AI product delivery is a KEY bear case item. Log as H5 evidence. Apple's AI moat not yet proven.

### Supply Chain — TSMC & Component Risks
**Source**: Apple 10-K (S01), CNBC/earnings call (S10)
**Signal**: CAUTION — tariff headwinds real
- Tariffs cost $1.1B in Q4 FY2025; $1.4B expected Q1 FY2026
- Section 232 semiconductor investigation — no additional tariffs yet but risk remains
- Manufacturing concentrated in China mainland — any escalation creates risk
- Apple shipping AI servers from Houston (new US manufacturing = tariff hedge)
**Divergence?**: Confirmed — reported figures include tariff impact. No grassroots signal diverges.
