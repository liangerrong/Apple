# 01 Research Plan

## Sub-Question Framework

### Q1 — What is Apple?
*History, organization, revenue composition, geographic/customer structure, core KPIs*

**Planned Queries**:
1. NotebookLM: "What are Apple's revenue segments and their FY2024 annual figures? Provide iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables, Services breakdown."
2. NotebookLM: "What is Apple's geographic revenue breakdown for FY2024? Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, Rest of Asia Pacific."
3. Tavily: "Apple FY2024 annual revenue by segment official results"

**Source types**: SEC filing (NotebookLM), financial news (Tavily)

---

### Q2 — How does the business work?
*Business processes, value chain position, cost structure, key resources*

**Source types**: SEC filing (NotebookLM), analyst research (Tavily)
